Race start card on the Wall

One card per unit, adults only, posted the day a board starts (the Cup on its first day; the monthly race on the 1st, or the day it comes back after a challenge). The line rotates from the bank of 20 and never repeats until the bank runs out. Everything else on the card comes from the competition itself: name, dates, scoring rules, prizes. Three options, each shown for the Christmas Cup and for a monthly race.

A · Outlined card (same family as Champions)
Champions use the amber frame, the start card uses the blue one. The line is the headline, the facts sit under it, "How to score" as chips.
